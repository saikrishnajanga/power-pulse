2. Execution Objectives
Functionality that will exist at the end of this milestone (end of Week 8):
Signed-in Telangana household users (and small businesses with a covered tariff category) can give consent, upload a bill (PDF or image), review and confirm the extracted details, and complete an appliance questionnaire.
The system calculates cost from the tariff, shows consumption history, forecasts monthly and seasonal usage with confidence, flags spikes with a likely cause, estimates a usage breakdown, and benchmarks against similar households.
Users see ranked recommendations (solar, battery, LED, inverter AC, solar water heater, behaviour changes) with subsidies applied, ranges, payback, 5 and 10-year savings, and environmental impact, all labelled as estimates with visible assumptions. They can compare options, compare bills, and download a PDF savings report.
Users can delete their data and account. Analysts can maintain tariff, subsidy, price and parameter data.
The system runs on staging and production, with logging, metrics, alerts, feature flags, rollback, and a completed pilot.
Acceptance criteria that will be met:
Every Gherkin acceptance criterion for FR-01 to FR-27 in PRISM-R Section 7 passes (traceability in Section 7 below).
Edge cases EC-01 to EC-19 behave as specified in PRISM-S Section 10.
The NFR targets in PRISM-R Section 9 are measured and met, or deviations are approved by the Decision Owner.
Success metrics can be measured from recorded data: 90% or higher bills read correctly, 15% or lower forecast error, 70% or higher users viewing a recommendation with ROI, 10% or more estimated savings per user.
3. Milestone Scope
Included
All in-scope items of PRISM-R Section 2 and all components of PRISM-S Section 5, except the optional smart meter provider integration.
Reference data sourcing (tariffs, subsidies, prices, parameters, benchmarks) and golden test cases.
Staging and production environments, CI/CD, monitoring, runbook, and rollback rehearsal.
Test suites in PRISM-S Section 11, accuracy evaluation, load and security testing, and a pilot with consenting users.
Documentation and the final presentation.
Explicit exclusions
Everything in PRISM-R Section 3 (installation or sales, payments, device control, financial advice, industrial users, regions beyond Telangana).
A real smart meter provider integration. The adapter (task SM1) is an optional stretch task and the first cut line.
Deep-learning forecasting or disaggregation, microservices, and native mobile apps (PRISM-I and PRISM-S non-goals).
